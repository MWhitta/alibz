**Method/claim.** Critical review of measured widths/shifts for 77 spectra (neutral and ionized atoms).

**Adoptable specifics.** Large tables (535 kB text); useful as lookup of w(1e17 cm^-3) for Fe I/II, Mg, Ca, Na, K, Al, H. Hydrogen discussion supports Halpha for n_e.

**Where it would enter alibz.** `stark.py` calibration of c4 against measured widths for the few lines whose widths exceed 0.03 nm (H, Li, Na D).

**Fit-quality test.** Real spectra with H alpha: n_e agreement and n_e-at-bound flag; expected: cleaner H alpha n_e values.

**Caveats.** Experimental, mostly 1e16-1e17 cm^-3, T 10-20 kK; Z300 resolution limits use.
