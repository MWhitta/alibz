**Method/claim.** McWhirter is necessary but not sufficient for transient, inhomogeneous laser plasmas; adds relaxation-time and diffusion-length conditions and recommends time/space-resolved checks, and that McWhirter alone not be used as proof of LTE.

**Adoptable specifics.** Only abstract and introduction were in the retrieved text (5.5 kB); relaxation-time and diffusion-length expressions (their Secs. 3-4) are NOT verified here. Practical rule: compare excitation relaxation time (and atom diffusion over it) to plasma evolution time.
