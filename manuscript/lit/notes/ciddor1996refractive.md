**Method/claim.** Updated Edlen-type dispersion for 300-1700 nm with CO2 and humidity terms.

**Adoptable specifics.** Dispersion: 1e8(n-1)=k1/(k0-s^2)+k3/(k2-s^2), k0=238.0185, k1=5792105, k2=57.362, k3=167917 (k1,k2,k3 seen in text; k0 from memory). Differences from Birch-Downs are ~1e-8 (sub-pm).

**Where it would enter alibz.** Same as birch1994refractive; NIR segment above 650 nm is where Ciddor is the stated authority.

**Fit-quality test.** Same.
