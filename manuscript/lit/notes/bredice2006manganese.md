**Method/claim.** SA coefficient from line-intensity ratios of lines sharing an upper level (branching ratio), no Stark data needed.

**Adoptable specifics.** Ratio of measured to theoretical branching ratio gives SA directly (abstract). Same idea as alibz doublets.

**Where it would enter alibz.** Generalise `invert_doublet_tau` to any pair sharing an upper level (Fe I multiplets).

**Fit-quality test.** Fe I partner-pair ratio consistency on Z300 means; K I 766/770 check.

**Caveats.** Pairs must be resolved at 0.1-0.2 nm; many Fe pairs blend.
