**Method/claim.** Effective Gaunt-factor formula for electron-impact width of ion lines; shifts from dispersion relation; shift/half-width ratio 0.7-1.4.

**Adoptable specifics.** Basis of alibz n_eff scaling in Eq. stark-quad. Not resolvable at Z300 resolution.

**Where it would enter alibz.** `stark.py` already uses scaling.

**Fit-quality test.** None practical.

**Caveats.** Applies to isolated lines only.
