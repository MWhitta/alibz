**Method/claim.** Optical depth from the measured SA gives columnar density in LTE, so concentration ratios follow without needing the thin lines.

**Adoptable specifics.** Column density N_col from tau and line parameters (abstract); T enters weakly.

**Where it would enter alibz.** `absorption.py`: report columnar density for saturated resonance lines as extra constraint in the K/Na concentrations.

**Fit-quality test.** K I 766/770 doublet check and alkali composition stability (the 14/38 swing in the stimulated-emission A/B).

**Caveats.** Homogeneous column assumed.
