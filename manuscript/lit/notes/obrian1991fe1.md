**Method/claim.** Fourier-transform branching fractions with laser-fluorescence lifetimes of 186 Fe I levels (25,900-60,758 cm^-1) give absolute transition probabilities to ~5% or better for many lines.

**Adoptable specifics.** Abstract-level: precise A for strong Fe I lines; the compilation (fuhr2006fe) is the practical source.

**Where it would enter alibz.** Use as an independent check of selected Fe I gA in the line DB.

**Fit-quality test.** Same Fe I Boltzmann consistency test as fuhr2006fe.

**Caveats.** Large dataset; incremental over NIST.
