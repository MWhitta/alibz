**Method/claim.** X-ray Al plasma at solid density (up to 180 eV) matches Ecker-Kroll, not Stewart-Pyatt.

**Adoptable specifics.** Regime n~1e23 cm^-3; irrelevant at 1e17 where both reduce to Debye-Huckel.

**Where it would enter alibz.** Supports choosing EK form in `sahaboltzmann.py` if IPD is added.

**Fit-quality test.** Same as ecker1963lowering.

**Caveats.** Strongly coupled dense plasma; no support at LIBS densities.
