**Method/claim.** Systematic escape-factor definition for Gaussian, Lorentzian and Holtsmark profiles, for slab and cylinder geometries, with upper/lower bounds for the source function.

**Adoptable specifics.** Escape factors differ by geometry and profile by tens of percent at tau~1-10; abstract-level only (4 kB retrieved).

**Where it would enter alibz.** `absorption.py` geometry choice (slab vs cylinder) as an A/B option.

**Fit-quality test.** Synthetic SA round trips; K I 766/770 doublet ratio vs thin. Expected modest gain.

**Caveats.** Needs full paper for formulas; plasma is closer to a column than an infinite slab.
