**Method/claim.** ChemCam body unit and calibration: PLS on 69 standards at 1.6-8.7 m; accuracy requirement +/-10% relative on major elements. Reports REP, cross-validated REP, RMSEP, CV-RMSEP.

**Adoptable specifics.** Table 8d medians of relative error (%), 1.6 m: Na2O 3.2, MgO 7.4, Al2O3 2.7, SiO2 0.78, P2O5 13.2, K2O 12.0, CaO 2.1, TiO2 6.1, MnO 2.5, Fe2O3 3.9; 3.0 m: 2.5, 4.2, 2.4, 0.69, 9.3, 8.8, 2.5, 7.0, 2.1, 3.7. Cross-validated errors worse for P, K, Ti, Mn. Relative error correlates with relative compositional range (R2 0.89).

**Where it would enter alibz.** Benchmark numbers in the report; no module change.

**Fit-quality test.** Compare suite S median relative error on majors vs 2-4% (in-sample PLS on Si, Al, Ca, Fe).

**Caveats.** Training-set (in-sample) PLS at 1.6-8.7 m, stand-off geometry and Mars-like atmosphere; not first-principles.
