**Method/claim.** Progress report on a line list (Kurucz) with predicted levels and lines for all ions, with atlases to test line data.

**Adoptable specifics.** No usable numbers for CF-LIBS; predicted gf for lines missing in NIST could fill partition sums and line lists, but with large uncertainties.

**Where it would enter alibz.** Possible source of missing weak lines for the confounder check in `detections.py`.

**Fit-quality test.** Count of unexplained peaks in Z300 Fe means that match Kurucz predicted lines; expected modest reduction in unmatched peaks.

**Caveats.** Predicted gf accuracy poorer than critical compilations; wavelength uncertainty can exceed 0.1 nm for predicted lines, comparable to Z300 pixel.
