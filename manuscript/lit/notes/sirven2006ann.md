**Method/claim**: PCA and neural networks classify three soils; NN with line areas as inputs gave about 5 % accuracy and precision for Cr.

**Adoptable specifics**: Inputs replaced by line areas reduced data and gave best results.

**Where it would enter alibz**: Supports peak-area inputs.

**Fit-quality test**: synthetic suite S (bench/synth_cases.py): area-input vs pixel-input model.

**Caveats**: One element, one soil.
