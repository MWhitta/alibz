**Method/claim.** Tabulated measured Stark widths w and shifts d of neutral-atom lines (n_e=1e16-1e17 cm^-3 reference, T 5-40 kK).

**Adoptable specifics.** Widths at n_e=1e16 cm^-3 are typically 0.002-0.05 nm; scale linearly with n_e (impact approximation). Supports alibz claim that predicted widths (~0.002 nm) are below the Z300 resolution.

**Where it would enter alibz.** `stark.py` coefficient table; low priority since the Stark channel is off by default.

**Fit-quality test.** Only useful for synthetic widths; real data cannot resolve it.

**Caveats.** Old data; ion-broadening correction needed; not resolvable at 0.1-0.2 nm.
